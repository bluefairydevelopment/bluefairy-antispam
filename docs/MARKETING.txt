BlueFairy Anti-Spam — Marketing Content
==========================================


3-SENTENCE SUMMARY
------------------

BlueFairy Anti-Spam silently blocks bot submissions on WordPress comments, user registration, WooCommerce reviews, My Account registration, and checkout — no CAPTCHAs, no puzzles, no friction for real visitors. An invisible honeypot field and a cryptographically signed time-trap token stop bots the moment they touch your forms, while an optional Stop Forum Spam integration cross-references known spammer IPs and emails from a global database. Always free, requires zero configuration, and protects all your forms from the moment you activate it — GPL-2.0-or-later, PHP 8.1+, WordPress 6.5+.
